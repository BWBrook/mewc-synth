## Failures and fixes this morning

- **Out of memory beside a neighbour:** the seven failures were relaunched at 23:38, with ConvNeXt V2-L alone
  on gpu-5.
- **`PYTORCH_CUDA_ALLOC_CONF=expandable_segments:True` is unusable on the Nectar GRID vGPU (A100D-40C):**
  `cuMemAddressReserve` returns CUDA_ERROR_NOT_SUPPORTED, so every run failed at model load. Relaunched
  without it.
- **Second-context launch failures:** on ngx-gpu, the second lane's runs failed for about six minutes with
  CUDA_ERROR_LAUNCH_FAILED at the first convolution while the first lane's EVA-02-B started up. Then a run
  started normally, and a foreground test (`CUDA_LAUNCH_BLOCKING=1`, tiny config) also trained cleanly as a
  second process. No Xid errors appeared, and the licence was valid. `queue_v2.sh` now retries a failed job
  once after two minutes (training resumes from its last checkpoint), and the four casualties were
  requeued.
- **ConvNeXt V2-L needs more than 40 GB at batch 64 even alone** (36.8 GB in use when it failed).
  `train.py` gains `grad_checkpointing` (default off). A CPU check with ConvNeXt V2-T and the top two stages
  trainable confirmed that all 130 trainable tensors still receive gradients. The run resumes from its
  completed frozen phase.
