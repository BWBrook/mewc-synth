# 2026-10-22 Classifier screen, round 1: look-alike translation at an 80% synthetic share

Run by Claude (Opus 5.5) on ngx-gpu-4 and ngx-gpu-5 (two Nectar A100 40C vGPUs). Barry approved the
screen in chat on 22 October 2026: "Yes, excellent plan, It's looking promising, for sure, and I
approve of your recommendation. Let's do it!"

## Design as run

- Classifier: patched `mewc-train` (branch `fix/progressive-training-and-speed`) in
  `zaandahl/mewc-train:2.0.9`, `VTB` (ViT-B/16, 384 px), `CLASS_SAMPLES_DEFAULT=400`, 3 frozen
  epochs, progressive stages of 3 epochs to 12, batch 16. All 96 model classes, train capped at
  4,000 per class (seeded), shared validation (100 per class), scored on every split_v1 test snip of
  the model classes (78,371). About 77 min per run on ngx-gpu-4 and 95 min on ngx-gpu-5, whose
  GPU steps are 18% slower at the same clocks (probably a shared physical GPU; not visible from the
  guest).
- Pseudo-rare classes: spotted-tailed quoll, forester kangaroo, black currawong and black rat, cut
  to 50 real training snips (nested subset, at most 5 per near-duplicate group). Their test sets are
  2,000 snips each.
- Arms:
  - **A0**: real only. Seeds 1, 2, 3.
  - **S80** (run name `A1`): A0 plus 200 translated snips per pseudo-rare class, so 80% of that
    class's sampling draws are synthetic. SDXL img2img with the class's Stage B adapter, strength
    0.65, guidance 3.5, 30 steps, 512 px, finished by `finish_snip` (Lanczos to 600, JPEG q75, with
    the class's q95 share). Sources are real camera-trap train snips of the look-alike: eastern
    quoll, Bennett's wallaby, grey currawong, swamp rat. Seeds 1, 2, 3.
  - **R**: pseudo-rare classes at their full real training data (cap 4,000), as the headroom
    reference. Seeds 1, 2.

**Deviations from `PLAN.md`.** S80 is the plan's A2 at the 80% share; the run name `A1` clashes
with the plan's A1 (copy-paste), which has not been run. The plan's 50% share (S50) is running now.
R is an addition. The plan's quality check 4 (shortcut probe) should have run before any classifier
comparison; it ran afterwards, below. The n = 20 subsets have not been run.

## Results (all test snips; camera-trap-only results agree within 0.01)

F1 by class, seed means:

| Class | A0 | S80 | R |
|---|---|---|---|
| spotted-tailed quoll | 0.930 | 0.940 | 0.982 |
| forester kangaroo | 0.923 | 0.914 | 0.986 |
| black currawong | 0.769 | 0.675 | 0.935 |
| black rat | 0.754 | 0.760 | 0.945 |
| macro-F1, all 96 | 0.916 | 0.915 | 0.936 |

S80 minus A0, with 95% intervals from a paired bootstrap over test snips (models fixed), then over
test snips and seed runs:

| Metric | Difference | Test snips | Test snips and seeds |
|---|---|---|---|
| quoll F1 | +0.009 | [+0.004, +0.015] | [-0.009, +0.031] |
| kangaroo F1 | -0.010 | [-0.016, -0.003] | [-0.030, +0.010] |
| black currawong F1 | -0.094 | [-0.107, -0.081] | [-0.169, -0.015] |
| black rat F1 | +0.006 | [-0.004, +0.016] | [-0.034, +0.052] |
| macro-F1, other 92 | -0.000 | [-0.002, +0.002] | [-0.006, +0.005] |

R minus A0 is +0.05 to +0.19 F1 for the four classes, every interval excluding zero under both
bootstraps: 50 real snips leave a large gap, and that gap is what synthetic data would have to
close.

Where the test snips go (share of predictions, pooled over seeds):

- Real black currawongs called grey currawong: A0 26.4%, S80 37.3%, R 5.6%. Grey currawongs called
  black: 2.2%, 1.8%, 5.2%. S80 made the classifier less willing to call a real bird black currawong.
- Spotted-tailed quolls called eastern quoll: 7.8%, 4.8%, 1.5%. Bennett's wallaby confusions for
  forester kangaroo and the black rat confusions barely moved.

**Decision under the pre-registered rule: S80 fails.** No pseudo-rare class gains with an interval
excluding zero once seed variation is included, and black currawong loses 0.09 F1.

### The no-harm rule is too strict as written

The plan's "no abundant class loses more than 1 point of F1" flags 16 of 92 non-focus classes for
S80, but also 11 for R, which differs from A0 only in the four focus classes and so cannot harm the
others; the flags are seed and sampling noise. The two S80 flags that look real are knock-on effects
of the black currawong shift: grey currawong (-0.084; its recall is unchanged at 0.96, its precision
falls because more real black currawongs are labelled grey) and rakali (-0.087 on 74 test snips,
about two snips). A noise-calibrated rule is proposed in `PLAN.md`; it is written after seeing these
results and is labelled as such.

## Identity of the translated snips (VTL judge)

| Class | Judged intended | Main wrong call |
|---|---|---|
| black currawong | 79% | grey currawong 21% |
| black rat | 57% | swamp rat 28% |
| forester kangaroo | 87% | Bennett's wallaby 13% |
| spotted-tailed quoll | 37% | eastern quoll 63% |

Judge identity did not predict usefulness: the quoll snips (37%) were harmless to slightly helpful,
the currawong snips (79%) harmful. No copies: nearest-neighbour cosine to the 50 training snips had
no values at or above 0.90; pairwise diversity 0.62 to 0.75 against 0.46 to 0.51 for real test snips.

## Shortcut probe (PLAN quality check 4, run after the comparison)

`src/shortcut_probe.py`: a small CNN on high-pass residuals (image minus Gaussian blur, sigma 2) of
snips after the classifier's input path (600 px JPEG, bilinear to 384), five-fold grouped
cross-validation, held-out AUC (0.5 = indistinguishable).

| Mode | What differs | AUC |
|---|---|---|
| pair | translated snip vs its own real source (species changes) | 0.79 (folds 0.80 to 0.85) |
| within | translated vs real train snips of the same target class | 0.71 (black currawong 0.83, quoll 0.86, rat 0.66, kangaroo 0.57) |
| vae | real snip vs its own round trip through 512 px, the SDXL VAE and `finish_snip` | 0.69 |
| resize | real snip vs 600 -> 512 -> 600 px and JPEG q75, no VAE | 0.50 |

The 512 px resolution and the JPEG finishing leave nothing detectable; the SDXL VAE decoder (and
diffusion on top of it) leaves a fingerprint. Within black currawong, synthetic snips are separable
from real ones at AUC 0.83 on high-frequency content alone, the shortcut that best explains S80's
harm there: with 80% of draws synthetic, the class can be learned partly as "the generator's
texture", and real black currawongs without it fall to grey currawong, which has 4,000 real
examples. The within-class probe also carries scene differences (the sources come from grey
currawong sites), so it bounds the fingerprint from above.
