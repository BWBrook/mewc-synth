# 2026-10-23 Classifier screen, round 2: synthetic share, and the bird arm

Run overnight 22 to 23 October by Claude (Opus 5.5) on ngx-gpu-4 and ngx-gpu-5, under the design in
`logs/2026-10-22-screen-r1.md` and the `PLAN.md` change-log entries of 22 October (S50, S20 and the
bird arm were each registered before their results). Evaluation: `src/screen_eval.py`, 1,000
bootstrap draws; "seeds" intervals also resample seed runs within each arm.

## Synthetic share (pseudo-rare classes at 50 real snips)

The same 200 translated snips per pseudo-rare class at three shares of the class's sampling draws,
set by linking each real snip k times: S80 (k = 1), S50 (k = 4), S20 (k = 16). In S20, 320 of the
400 draws per epoch are real and 80 synthetic, against 400 real in A0, so the comparison isolates
replacing a fifth of the real draws with synthetic ones. Three seeds per arm.

F1 by class, seed means, all test snips (camera-trap only within 0.01):

| Arm | quoll | kangaroo | black currawong | black rat | focus macro-F1 | other 92 classes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A0 | 0.930 | 0.923 | 0.769 | 0.755 | 0.844 | 0.919 |
| S20 | 0.958 | 0.928 | 0.817 | 0.802 | 0.876 | 0.922 |
| S50 | 0.946 | 0.921 | 0.734 | 0.820 | 0.855 | 0.923 |
| S80 | 0.940 | 0.914 | 0.675 | 0.760 | 0.822 | 0.919 |
| R (full real) | 0.983 | 0.983 | 0.940 | 0.944 | 0.963 | 0.936 |

Differences from A0, 95% intervals over test snips and seeds:

| Metric | S20 | S50 | S80 |
|---|---|---|---|
| focus macro-F1 | **+0.032 [+0.008, +0.054]** | +0.011 [-0.014, +0.033] | -0.022 [-0.054, +0.012] |
| quoll F1 | **+0.027 [+0.017, +0.036]** | **+0.016 [+0.008, +0.024]** | +0.009 [-0.008, +0.030] |
| kangaroo F1 | +0.004 [-0.015, +0.023] | -0.003 [-0.019, +0.013] | -0.010 [-0.029, +0.010] |
| black currawong F1 | +0.048 [-0.013, +0.110] | -0.036 [-0.098, +0.028] | **-0.094 [-0.166, -0.022]** |
| black rat F1 | +0.048 [-0.002, +0.098] | **+0.065 [+0.017, +0.112]** | +0.006 [-0.035, +0.050] |
| macro-F1, other 92 | +0.003 [-0.003, +0.008] | +0.004 [-0.002, +0.009] | -0.000 [-0.006, +0.006] |

- The gains come through recall; precision is unchanged at 0.98 to 1.00. S20 raises recall for
  quoll from 0.870 to 0.920, black currawong 0.637 to 0.701 and black rat 0.609 to 0.676, and cuts
  look-alike confusion (quoll to eastern quoll 7.8% to 3.1%; black currawong to grey currawong or
  forest raven 29.8% to 24.2%).
- Black currawong is monotonic in share: +0.048 at 20%, -0.036 at 50%, -0.094 at 80%. That fits the
  round 1 diagnosis (a VAE fingerprint the classifier can exploit when synthetic snips dominate a
  class) and shows the same snips help when they are a minority.
- S20 recovers about a quarter of the gap to full real data (+0.032 of +0.118 in focus macro-F1).
- No harm: under the amended rule (a non-focus class is harmed if its two-level interval lies
  entirely below -0.01) S20 and S50 harm no class; S80 harms one (rakali, camera-trap snips only).
  R harms none, as it must.

**Under the pre-registered decision rule, S20 passes on the pooled pseudo-rare classes** (interval
excluding zero, no harm), and individually only for quoll. Caveats: S20 was added after S80's
result (registered before its own), so three shares were tried; and with three seeds per arm the
seed resampling is coarse. A0's seed 1 (focus macro-F1 0.871) matches S20's seeds (0.868 to
0.889); the difference comes from A0's seeds 2 and 3 (0.827, 0.835). Seeds 4 and 5 for A0 and S20,
an S10 arm, and the plan's production-model (VTL) check were launched on 23 October to confirm or
overturn it.

## Bird arm (registered 22 October, before results)

Nine photo-dominated birds weak on camera-trap snips. **B-syn**: R plus 400 translations per target of
its own camera-trap train snips (strength 0.55, domain adapter). **B-rep**: R plus 400 links cycling
through the same real camera-trap snips. Three seeds each; R seeds 1 to 3 as baseline. Judge
identity of the translations 77% (pink robin, 43 of 400 called flame/scarlet robin) to 99% (eagle).

Camera-trap test snips (about 450 over the nine targets; 11 to 147 per class), seed means:

| | R | B-syn | B-rep |
|---|---|---|---|
| focus macro-F1 | 0.793 | 0.779 | 0.788 |
| Pacific black duck | 0.683 | 0.730 | 0.802 |
| flame robin | 0.759 | 0.765 | 0.795 |
| wedge-tailed eagle | 0.902 | 0.936 | 0.946 |
| strong-billed honeyeater | 0.806 | 0.687 | 0.658 |
| crescent honeyeater | 0.813 | 0.751 | 0.751 |

B-syn minus R in focus macro-F1: -0.014 [-0.046, +0.020]; B-rep minus R: -0.005 [-0.047, +0.035].
**B-syn fails** (it beats neither R nor B-rep). Up-weighting the scarce real camera-trap snips, with or
without the generator, helps the duck and the eagle (B-rep duck +0.122 [+0.023, +0.239], eagle
+0.044 [+0.005, +0.093]) and hurts the honeyeaters, whose camera-trap snips are few (63 to 203) and
confusable with each other; the net is nil. No class is harmed under the amended rule.

The pre-registered shortcut probe for the bird translations was meant to run before the classifier
runs; it ran out of GPU memory overnight (7,200 images held on the GPU) and was rerun on 23 October
with images held in CPU memory (the change reproduces the round 1 pair result, 0.80 against 0.79).
Translation against its own source: **AUC 0.975** (0.95 to 0.99 by class), against 0.80 for the
look-alike translations. With source and target the same species, processing is the only
difference; and small birds are small detector crops upscaled to 600 px, so the real snips are soft
while SDXL paints in fine detail they never have. The classifier could separate these synthetic
birds from real ones almost perfectly, which is consistent with B-syn adding nothing over B-rep.
Any future camera-trap synthesis for small, upscaled subjects should match the source's effective
resolution (for example, degrade the output to the source crop's native size before finishing).

## What this means

- Look-alike translation works as a minority supplement for a class cut to 50 real snips (S20), not
  as a replacement for real data, and the right share is near or below 20%. The S10 arm and the
  extra seeds will say whether the optimum is lower and whether the S20 gain holds.
- Same-species camera-trap translation adds nothing beyond what re-weighting the real camera-trap
  snips gives, at least at 400 per class on top of full real data.
