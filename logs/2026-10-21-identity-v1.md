## Findings

1. Fully trained classes are generated with the right identity (0.92 to
   1.00).
2. At n=50, classes with a close, abundant look-alike collapse onto it.
   Synthetic spotted-tailed quolls are, to the judge, entirely eastern
   quolls. That matches Barry's own reading earlier the same evening ("mostly
   Eastern quolls, but not quite, the face isn't exactly right"). Distinctive
   classes (forester kangaroo) survive n=50 intact.
3. Higher guidance (7.5) does not help.
4. **Negative prompts raise the judge's score but not true identity.** The
   sheet shows the black-currawong "fixes" are mostly extreme bill or eye
   close-ups, blurry fragments and odd crops. The kangaroo negatives become
   deer- or llama-like hybrids. The negative prompt pushes generation away
   from typical images rather than toward diagnostic features. The judge can
   be satisfied by style or source cues (close-up yellow-eye frames resemble
   the `cu` currawong close-ups). Not adopted.
5. Black rat stays ambiguous (rodent bodies without scale) under every
   setting, as expected for the scale-limited negative control.

## Consequences for the design

- **The ViT-L is a development instrument, not the experiment's filter.** It
  has seen thousands of real pseudo-rare snips, including `split_v1` test
  images, an oracle that does not exist for genuinely rare species. Any
  filter applied to synthetic data in the classifier comparison must be
  trained only on the same restricted data (train split, pseudo-rare classes
  at n=50 or 20).
- The fix for look-alike collapse must come from training. Next: a Stage B
  per-class LoRA on top of Stage A, trained on the rare class's n real snips
  paired with an equal number of look-alike snips, each with its own
  caption, so the adapter learns the contrast. Judge again with the ViT-L
  and Barry's blind review.

## Queued overnight: identity of Stage A v2 (`work/identity_v2.sh`)

Starts automatically when v2 training ends. It generates 64 snips each:

- Pseudo-rare and control classes, plain guidance 5 (the v1 comparison).
- **Camera-trap captions for photo-dominated classes**, at the IR share of
  their camera-trap snips. These are the domain-transfer test. The classes
  and their camera-trap training snips:
  - flame_robin: 268
  - scrubtit: **15**
  - strong_billed_honeyeater: 63
  - tasmanian_boobook: 230 (IR 50%)

Snips are then scored with the ViT-L, and the report is written to
`identity_v2/report.txt`. `identity_report.py` now maps the model's name
aliases (`flame_robin` is `flame_scarlet_robin`).
