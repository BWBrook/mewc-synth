## ViT pooling fix (01:47 UTC)

The first race preflight (deep) failed five candidates on harness bugs, and its key check exposed a
parity problem: with `global_pool="avg"`, timm drops a class-token ViT's pretrained final norm and puts a
freshly initialised norm after pooling. kimm (mewc-train) applies the pretrained final norm to every token
and averages them. `mewc-torch` now builds such ViTs with the pretrained norm kept and no post-pool norm
(timm's `fc_norm=False`, or `use_fc_norm=False` for Eva); its features equal the mean of the normalised
patch tokens exactly (kimm also averages in the class token, 1 of 577). Checkpoints trained with a
post-pool norm (EVA-02) keep theirs. Other fixes: the compression layer reads timm's `head_hidden_size`
(MobileNetV4, EfficientViT), and MetaFormer (CAFormer), which lacks a group matcher, is grouped by stage.
SigLIP 2 entries switched to the checkpoints trained with average pooling (`*_siglip_gap_384.v2_webli`).
ConvNeXt builds are unchanged. The three `mewc-torch` ViT-B seed-1 runs, 26 to 35 min in, were stopped
and requeued at the end of their lanes; the ConvNeXt-B and mewc-train runs continue.
