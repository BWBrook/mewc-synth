## Label check

After the parity lanes finish, each instance scores the test and held-out-project lists with its
`mewc-torch` runs (`ops/2026-10-29/labelcheck_score.sh`, output in `v2_runs/labelcheck`), and
`src/label_check.py` lists confident disagreements across the 12-run ensemble.

## Race preflight (deep, CPU)

`src/mewc_torch/preflight.py` builds each of the 30 race candidates with the MEWC head at its race size,
reports parameters, body groups and the share its unfreeze setting trains, checks the ONNX export
against PyTorch and times it in `onnxruntime` on 8 threads. Transformers can now train at a non-native
size (`img_size` passed to timm, which resamples the position embeddings).
