## Domain transfer works

The v2 grid puts "camera trap snip of a flame or scarlet robin" (row 1) next
to "photograph of a flame or scarlet robin" (row 2). Row 1 shows birds on the
ground in leaf litter, with camera-trap blur, washed-out colour and low
angles. Row 2 shows perched birds with bokeh. The flame_robin training data
are 98% eBird photographs (268 camera-trap snips in train), yet the
camera-trap request yields camera-trap robins that the ViT-L identifies
correctly: **0.984 intended** (scarlet males, a flame male, and females).
The camera-trap style learned across other classes transfers to a
photo-dominated class.

## Reading

1. Source-aware captions give control over style. Camera-trap snips can be
   generated for classes trained almost entirely on photographs, which is
   where the production classifier's training distribution least matches
   deployment.
2. Look-alike collapse is the central remaining problem, and it hits real
   low-count classes the same way as pseudo-rare ones: scrubtit falls onto
   Tasmanian scrubwren, and honeyeater and boobook partly onto look-alikes.
   Abundant classes (robin, controls) keep their identity.
3. Changing captions did not change the collapse (expected; same data and
   weighting).
