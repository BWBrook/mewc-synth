# 2026-10-25 Round 4: the S20 gain transfers to VTL; model-size pilot

Runs on ngx-gpu-4 and ngx-gpu-5, 24 October 00:08 to 25 October; registered in `PLAN.md` (24 October)
and `docs/roadmap.md` before their results; evaluated unattended on deep-server

## VTL, eight seeds per arm

Production-like VTL (ViT-L/16, 384 px), same screen recipe; A0 (pseudo-rare classes at 50 real
snips) against S20 (plus 200 look-alike translations per class at a 20% share). About 2.8 to 3.6 h
per run.

| | A0 | S20 |
|---|---|---|
| focus macro-F1, seed mean | 0.852 | 0.881 |
| focus macro-F1, seeds 1 to 8 | 0.833, 0.892, 0.820, 0.818, 0.815, 0.903, 0.864, 0.874 | 0.856, 0.876, 0.866, 0.884, 0.895, 0.889, 0.911, 0.871 |
| macro-F1, all 96 classes | 0.925 | 0.931 |

| focus macro-F1 | **+0.029 [+0.004, +0.053]** | **+0.028 [+0.003, +0.053]** |
| macro-F1, other 92 classes | +0.005 [+0.002, +0.008] | +0.006 [+0.001, +0.012] |
| black currawong F1 | +0.050 [-0.009, +0.106] | |
| black rat F1 | +0.050 [-0.010, +0.109] | |

No class harmed under the amended rule. **Under the criterion registered on 24 October, the S20 gain
transfers to the production model**: the same size as on VTB (+0.029), with a small gain on the other
92 classes as well, and S20 also narrows the seed-to-seed spread (lowest seed 0.856 against 0.815).
The two-seed check of round 3 (+0.004) was a draw from that spread, not a different answer.

## Model-size pilot (in progress)

Every class at its real data (R composition, cap 4,000), screen recipe, two seeds per model (VTB three),
timed with `src/bench_infer.py` on the A100 (batch 64) and on 8 CPU cores. Seed-1 results so far:

| Model | Params | macro-F1, all | macro-F1, camera-trap | A100 img/s | 8-core CPU img/s |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| VTL | 304 M | 0.949 | 0.921 | 108 | about 1 |
| VTB | 86 M | 0.937 | 0.909 | 186 | about 5 |
| ViT-S | 22 M | 0.930 | 0.898 | 308 | 12 |

The first pass of the EfficientNet and ConvNeXt runs failed at the first unfreeze: the screen script
passed `BUF=9` (blocks to unfreeze), right for ViTs, while these models expose three or four
blocks; mewc-train suggests 1 to 2 for them. Not a mewc-train fault (2.0.9 fails the same way).
`run_screen.sh` now takes `BUF`, and the eight convolutional models were relaunched on 25 October at
00:08 UTC with `BUF=2`, seed 1 for all first, then seed 2 where it fits before 11:00 UTC. The
8-core CPU timings above come from 32 images and vary up to twofold between machines; a 64-image
benchmark of every model follows the runs, and `src/pilot_summary.py` assembles the final table
(`screen/pilot_summary.csv`, copied to `deep-server:/data/mewc/screen_orch/out/`).

## Model-size pilot, final (25 October)

All 11 architectures, two seeds each (VTB three), R composition, screen recipe; blocks to unfreeze 9
for ViTs and 2 for EfficientNet and ConvNeXt; CPU figures from the 64-image benchmark on 8 cores
(JAX, random tensors: a proxy, not `mewc-predict`). Full table: `logs/2026-10-25-pilot-summary.csv`.

| Model | Params | macro-F1, all | macro-F1, camera-trap | A100 img/s | 8-core CPU img/s |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| VTL | 304 M | 0.949 | 0.921 | 108 | 1.7 |
| ConvNeXt-B | 88 M | 0.945 | 0.917 | 220 | 7.6 |
| ConvNeXt-S | 50 M | 0.937 | 0.901 | 268 | 11.3 |
| VTB | 86 M | 0.937 | 0.909 | 186 | 4.6 |
| ViT-S | 22 M | 0.930 | 0.898 | 308 | 18.4 |
| ConvNeXt-T | 28 M | 0.926 | 0.882 | 310 | 18.8 |
| EfficientNetV2-M | 54 M | 0.923 | 0.881 | 193 | 8.7 |
| EfficientNetV2-S | 21 M | 0.917 | 0.875 | 323 | 22.0 |
| EfficientNetV2-B2 | 9.5 M | 0.894 | 0.841 | 713 | 50.3 |
| ConvNeXt-Pico | 8.7 M | 0.894 | 0.847 | 565 | 46.8 |
| EfficientNetV2-B0 | 6.6 M | 0.876 | 0.826 | 880 | 84.1 |

Seed ranges are narrow (at most 0.015). At these defaults ConvNeXt-B comes within 0.004 of VTL at
twice the A100 throughput and four to five times the CPU throughput, and ConvNeXt-S matches VTB while
being faster on both. The convolutional models train only two blocks here, so the per-family search
in the booking plan may lift them further; this ranking is a starting point, not a verdict.
