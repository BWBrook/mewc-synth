# 2026-10-24 Classifier screen, round 3: confirmation, a second synthetic set, S10, and VTL

Runs on ngx-gpu-4 and ngx-gpu-5 from 23 October 23:38 to 24 October 17:16 UTC, all registered in
`PLAN.md` (23 October entries) before their results. Scores were merged and evaluated unattended by
`ops/2026-10-23/orchestrate.sh` on deep-server (outputs also in
`deep-server:/data/mewc/screen_orch/out/`). Intervals: 1,000 bootstrap draws over test snips and seed
runs ("two-level").

## Seven VTB seeds, S10 and a second synthetic set

Focus = the four pseudo-rare classes at 50 real snips. A0 and S20 now have seven seeds each; S10,
S50, S80 and R three; **S20b** is a fresh synthetic draw (seed 52, newly sampled look-alike sources,
same adapters) at the 20% share, three seeds.

S20 per class over seven seeds: quoll +0.012 [-0.000, +0.024], kangaroo +0.016 [-0.002, +0.036],
black currawong +0.037 [-0.010, +0.087], black rat +0.049 [+0.001, +0.098]. S20b: kangaroo +0.037
[+0.020, +0.056], others positive with intervals spanning zero.

- **S20 is confirmed on VTB** under the registered criterion: the focus gain keeps an interval
  excluding zero over seven seeds, and no class is harmed under the amended rule (S10, S20 and S20b
  harm none; S50 harms chestnut teal and grey currawong). Other classes do not lose: +0.004
  [-0.000, +0.007] for S20.
- **It replicates on an independent synthetic draw** (S20b, +0.032), so it is not a property of one
  batch of generated snips.
- **The share curve peaks near 20%**: 10% helps less, 50% about breaks even, 80% harms (black
  currawong -0.092 [-0.152, -0.031]).
- The size of the effect: about a quarter of the gain that the full real data gives (+0.029 of
  +0.115).
