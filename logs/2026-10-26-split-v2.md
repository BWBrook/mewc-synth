# 2026-10-26 Split v2 over the full library

Built on deep-server with `src/split_v2.py` (CPU, 3 minutes, 16 GB RAM) from the full-library
curation outputs (`/data/mewc/curation/v1/`: index, 0.90 groups, thinning flags, DINOv2 embeddings).
Outputs in `deep-server:/data/mewc/curation/v2/` (`split.parquet`, `summary_by_class.csv`,
`summary.json`). Seed 20261026. Design from `docs/plan-next-booking.md` after its independent review.

## Steps

1. The 96 model classes: 3,989,662 snips.
2. Exact duplicates (same size and CRC32): 313 copies across classes dropped (conflicting labels);
   47,521 repeats within classes dropped, one copy kept.
3. Project-disjoint test stratum: the source projects `mtwt` (Matt Taylor Wild Tracker; 12.6k snips,
   54 classes) and `hr` (61.8k snips, 44 classes; the field cameras of the MEWC paper's case study),
   except in classes where they hold over 20% of the snips: 72,175 snips.
4. Validation and test per class by whole 0.90 groups of at most 200 snips, at most 20 snips per group
   (the rest of a drawn group excluded: 1,594 snips). Quotas: test min(2,000, 20%), validation
   min(500, 10%). Test 98,060, validation 29,018.
5. Purge of training snips within cosine 0.88 of any validation or test snip of their class: 154,070.
6. Training pool: training snips capped at 40,000 per class (thinning-kept snips first, then a seeded
   random fill): 1,011,682 snips, about 47 GB.

## The purge threshold

The review proposed 0.85. At 0.85 the purge removed 587,498 training snips, including 79% of
white-faced heron, 78% of echidna, 76% of Pacific black duck and 61% of red fox (955 left). A contact
sheet of purged training snips beside their nearest validation or test snip in the 0.85 to 0.90 band
showed mostly different animals in similar poses (separate eBird photos of ducks and herons, close-ups
of echidna spines); a few red fox pairs at 0.87 to 0.90 looked like the same camera site. Because the
0.90 groups already keep every pair at or above 0.90 on one side, the purge is a buffer, and 0.88
keeps the near-burst pairs out of training without discarding look-alikes:

| Purge threshold | Training snips purged |
|---|---|
| 0.85 | 587,498 |
| 0.88 | 154,070 |
| 0.90 | 34,346 (cross-project duplicates and fp16 rounding at the group threshold) |

At 0.88 echidna still loses 59% (it has long static sequences; its largest 0.95 group is 6,228 frames),
common bronzewing 33%, white-faced heron 30%, the rest 25% or less. The 25 low-count classes lose 3 to
40% (skink 40%) and keep 65 to 599 training snips.
